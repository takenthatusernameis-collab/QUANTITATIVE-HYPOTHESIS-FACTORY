## Working agreements for future sessions

1. **Read `next_agent_handoff.md` first**, then the register and shortlist, before generating anything new.
2. **Preserve prior work.** Add to the register; do not silently delete, relabel, or renumber existing IDs.
3. **ID discipline.** Ideas are `IDEA-0NN` (next free: `IDEA-017`; `IDEA-016` is **reserved and unused**), sources are `S-0NN` (next free: `S-458`; **only** `S-210 … S-219` is **reserved and unused** — `S-281 … S-290` are **live entries**, the ten Cycle-58 document-record entries, and the corpus is **447 entries of which 446 are verifiable**, S-290 being struck), validation specs are `V-0NN` (next free: `V-013`; `V-011` is **reserved and unused**, `V-010` is written and marked **never-to-execute**, and `V-012` is written and **executable** — the register's first *state-free* specification), rejections are `R-0NN` (next free: `R-076`), parkings are `P-0NN` (next free: `P-015`; `P-013` is **reserved and unused**), source gaps are `G-NN` (next free: `G-39`; `G-17` and `G-26` were skipped by the sequence and have never been used), and cycles are `Cycle N` (next: **Cycle 173**).
4. **Never invent a citation.** Every external claim in a dossier points to an `S-0NN` entry in the source ledger, and every ledger entry records how it was verified. If a claim cannot be sourced, mark it as an unresolved conjecture in the dossier.
5. **Scores are research-priority weights, not success probabilities.** No numerical probability of profit is attached to any candidate; the rubric score is a transparent weighted total out of 100.
6. **Theory only.** No backtests, no data acquisition or processing, no executable strategy code, no performance metrics, no workflow/test/infra edits, no cross-repository work. Unresolvable questions are documented and handed off, not silently relaxed.
7. **Every session ends with an updated handoff** naming the best next research task, the highest-value unresolved theoretical questions, and the next mechanism families to investigate.
