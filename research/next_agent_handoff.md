## 2. Highest-priority unresolved theoretical questions

1. **G-28 (IDEA-004) — the convergence-crowd identity limb, now with a structural reason.** The question
   is no longer "is there a published crowding measure" — there are many — but "is there a published
   measure of a **paired, netted** crowd". Cycle 58's contribution is the reason: every published
   position-disclosure source is **security-level and single-sided**, because it is built from *disclosed
   aggregate positions in securities*, while a convergence crowd is defined by a **pair** of positions
   (long one leg, short the other). The same disclosed long holding may be a convergence leg or a
   directional bet, and the short leg is invisible or contaminated. This null does not depend on further
   searching, and the next agent should not re-run the same search.
2. **G-21 (IDEA-011) — the first-adopter/second-adopter drift contrast, now confirmed *unused* rather
   than merely unrun.** Three cycles have established the null (Cycles 37, 52, 58). The sharper form:
   the field's largest comparability study (S-294) is a **first-time-adopter design by construction** and
   its outcomes do not include drift, and the nearest published outcome family (S-295) is likewise
   first-time-adopter. So the comparison would be a **contribution, not a replication** — which raises the
   candidate's novelty reading, lowers its evidence reading, and changes what a null in that design would
   mean. **This is a hand-off to validation, not a new search.** It cannot be run inside the IFRS 18
   window (G-32 limb (a) closed); it would need another standard, or a different outcome family.
