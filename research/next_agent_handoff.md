## 2. Highest-priority unresolved theoretical questions

1. **G-27 (IDEA-010) — the effective sample and the state frequency.** G-23 closed in the candidate's
   favour, and the price of that closure is an honest one: the informative units are (ETF, filing-date)
   state observations and the redemption days inside them, and that count is small. Two questions
   remain: how many such observations the rule can actually generate, and whether a *broker-dealer's*
   regulatory capital ratio is publicly available at the firm level (the bank-holding-company arm is
   FR Y-9C plus periodic reports, S-225/S-226). If the count is too small for inference, the correct
   verdict is **"untested"**, not a kill, and the candidate must not be re-specified to manufacture a
   larger sample.
2. **G-28 (IDEA-004) — which crowd is the state?** The candidate's state is a position-concentration
   measure of the *convergence/arbitrage* crowd in its own universe. The published neighbours (S-026's
   Days-ADV over all 13F institutions, S-157's comomentum, S-222's anomaly-level crowding) measure
   different crowds at different frequencies and are **not** substitutes. Until the measure is
   specified, V-002 cannot be amended or unfrozen.
3. **G-21 (IDEA-011) — the first-adopter versus second-adopter drift comparison.** A comparability
   clock predicts the drift increase in first-time adopters and its absence or reversal in
   second-time adopters; a generic "IFRS changes the numbers" effect predicts both move together. No
   study located runs that comparison on drift. A hit would be decisive; a null would leave the
   candidate with **no** published case in which post-adoption drift increased under a comparability
   clock, and that should be recorded plainly.
4. **IDEA-014's state-free existence limb** — the only untested claim in the register that is runnable
   without a new state: does the RFS compliance deadline move prices at all, and is the effect larger
   than the venue's round-trip cost (S-133)? A bounded search either closes the limb or justifies
   retiring the candidate.
5. **G-01 (IDEA-002, parked)** — no public continuous dealer funding-value-adjustment series. Unchanged
   and not dated; the blocker has no known end date, so it should not absorb further effort.
