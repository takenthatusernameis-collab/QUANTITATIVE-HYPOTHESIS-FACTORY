## 2. Highest-priority unresolved theoretical questions

1. **G-33 (IDEA-011) — is the pre-adoption impact disclosure quantifiable, comparable and available
   ex ante at firm level?** This is now the register's highest-value open question, because it is the
   only thing standing between the rank-1 candidate and a state that relieves its binding hard stop.
   The state exists in law (IAS 8 paragraphs 30–31; ESMA's Public Statement on the Implementation of
   IFRS 18, S-267) but the *mandated* disclosure is qualitative ("known or reasonably estimable
   information") and the quantified expected operating-profit change is only **encouraged**. Three
   sub-questions with stated answer routes: **(a) quantification rate** — what share of IFRS 18
   reporters disclose a *quantified* expected effect, in what unit, in the 2026 interim and annual
   reports; **(b) comparability** — is the disclosed effect defined consistently across issuers (same
   definition of "operating profit" before and after IFRS 18, same statement, same currency, same
   period); **(c) ex-ante availability and lag** — is it public before the trade's decision date and at
   what frequency, given that the interim-report filing calendar differs by jurisdiction.

   disclosures is public). **Kill condition:** if the disclosure is unquantified or inconsistently defined
