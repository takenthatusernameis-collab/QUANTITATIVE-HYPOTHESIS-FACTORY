     ask whether it requires an ETF to publish the *identities* of its authorised participants — on its
     website, or in its prospectus — and whether any exchange listing application (an
     `SR-NASDAQ-…` / `SR-NYSE-…` rule filing on sec.gov) or the Form N-1A "Creation and Redemption
     Procedures" appendix does. **If such a requirement exists, the route is regulatory rather than
     voluntary**, and the next step is to check whether sponsors comply and for which series.

     PDFs (fact sheets, "ETF liquidity"/"creation and redemption" pages) for a participant list.
